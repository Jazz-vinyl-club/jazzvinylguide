# The Atomic Mr. Basie on Vinyl: The Definitive Pressing Guide

## Summary

*The Atomic Mr. Basie* was recorded on October 21 and 22, 1957 at Capitol Studios in New York, with recording supervision by Teddy Reig. It was Count Basie's first release for the new Roulette label after he left Verve. Neal Hefti wrote and arranged all eleven tracks on the original LP. Roulette issued it in 1958 as R-52003 in mono.

Two things complicate buying this record. The packaging uses three different titles, and Discogs lists 144 versions, including pressings with tracks missing or resequenced. The 1994 CD *The Complete Atomic Basie* adds five tracks. Some later vinyl follows that edit rather than the 1958 one, and at least one reissue resequences the sides.

**Best early pressing for audiophiles:** An original [Roulette R-52003](https://www.discogs.com/release/2000393) mono pressing.

**Best modern pressing for audiophiles:** The [Classic Records 200g QUIEX SV-P](https://www.discogs.com/release/3080100), with Bernie Grundman's mastering confirmed by the runout etching.

**Best-value modern:** A clearly labelled repress of the original eleven-track program. Check the track list before buying anything unfamiliar.

## Title variants

The front cover reads "BASIE," styled as E=MC². The rear cover, spine and labels read "The Atomic Mr. Basie." The nickname comes from the mushroom-cloud cover photograph.

All three names refer to the same album. A seller listing a copy as "Basie" or "E=MC²" is not describing a different record.

## Recording history and tape provenance

Eddie "Lockjaw" Davis takes substantial solo space throughout. The trumpet section is Thad Jones, Joe Newman, Wendell Culley and Snooky Young, and it carries much of the record's character. Frank Foster shares tenor duties, with Al Grey, Benny Powell and Henry Coker on trombones.

Part of the second day went to material that never appeared on the 1958 LP. Two Jimmy Mundy arrangements, "Silks and Satins" and "Sleepwalker's Serenade," were recorded alongside a version of "The Late, Late Show."

Those tracks first appeared in 1994 on [The Complete Atomic Basie](https://www.discogs.com/release/1064725-Count-Basie-The-Complete-Atomic-Basie), an EMI CD running 56:34 against the LP's 39:30. Its bonus tracks sit at the end, but collector reports describe later editions built on this program with a different running order.

## Original Roulette pressings (1958)

[Roulette R-52003](https://www.discogs.com/release/2000393) is the US mono first pressing, carrying the original eleven tracks in their original sequence. Condition and price vary widely, and the record was popular enough that clean copies take searching.

Roulette also issued a stereo edition as SR-52003. Discogs lists [further 1958 US pressings](https://www.discogs.com/release/372502-Count-Basie-The-Atomic-Mr-Basie) with the same catalogue number. No published comparison of the mono and stereo originals has been found.

## UK pressings

EMI issued the album in the UK on Columbia under licence from Roulette. The [1958 mono](https://www.discogs.com/release/1207884-Count-Basie-The-Atomic-Mr-Basie) has green and gold labels with a grooved edge, in a laminated flip-back sleeve.

The [first UK stereo issue](https://www.discogs.com/release/13261336-Count-Basie-The-Atomic-Mr-Basie) uses mint green labels and predates the black and blue label version. A [later UK mono](https://www.discogs.com/release/20011345-Count-Basie-The-Atomic-Mr-Basie) carries a purchase tax code dating it to about 1960 or 1961.

At least one UK copy is missing "Double-O" and "Midnite Blue," according to a Discogs commenter who owned it. Check the track count on any UK pressing before buying.

## Modern reissues

The [Classic Records 200g QUIEX SV-P pressing](https://www.discogs.com/release/3080100) has runouts etched "R-52003-A BG" and "R-52003 B BG." The "BG" is Bernie Grundman's runout signature, which confirms the mastering credit from the record itself.

A 180g reissue catalogued JWR 4500 appears on the [Discogs master](https://www.discogs.com/master/303975-Count-Basie-Orchestra-The-Atomic-Mr-Basie). Owner comments report a different track sequence from the original, with four tracks moved between sides. It is not the 1958 program in its 1958 order.

A [2019 180g coloured-vinyl edition](https://www.discogs.com/release/13406201-Count-Basie-The-Atomic-Mr-Basie), catalogue 144489.1151.009, is listed as orange. One owner reports receiving white vinyl with heavy surface noise. A [2009 180g reissue](https://www.discogs.com/release/2683284-Count-Basie-The-Atomic-Mr-Basie) also exists, with no published review found.

## Buyer's guide by budget

**$$:** A [2009 180g reissue](https://www.discogs.com/release/2683284-Count-Basie-The-Atomic-Mr-Basie) or a clearly labelled later repress, after checking the track list against the original eleven.

**$$$:** A clean UK Columbia pressing, either the [1958 mono](https://www.discogs.com/release/1207884-Count-Basie-The-Atomic-Mr-Basie) or the [first stereo](https://www.discogs.com/release/13261336-Count-Basie-The-Atomic-Mr-Basie), with both "Double-O" and "Midnite Blue" present.

**$$$$:** The [Classic Records 200g QUIEX SV-P](https://www.discogs.com/release/3080100), or a clean original [Roulette R-52003](https://www.discogs.com/release/2000393).
