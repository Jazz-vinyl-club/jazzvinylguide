# Blues & Roots on Vinyl: The Definitive Pressing Guide

## Summary

*Blues & Roots* was recorded February 4, 1959 at Atlantic Studios in New York City, produced by Nesuhi Ertegun and engineered by Tom Dowd. Released March 1960 as Atlantic 1305 (mono) / SD 1305 (stereo), the album was Charles Mingus's direct response to critics who said his increasingly experimental music didn't swing – Ertegun had suggested he record a pure blues album. Mingus assembled a nine-piece group, including Jackie McLean, John Handy, Booker Ervin, and Pepper Adams on saxophones, with Horace Parlan and Mal Waldron splitting piano duties. All six original tracks are Mingus compositions; a 1998 Rhino CD reissue added four alternate takes not on the original LP, later carried onto at least one Rhino vinyl reissue as well.

This is a hard-driving nine-piece hard bop session with a lot of horn density, so pressing quality matters more than usual for how well individual horns separate versus blur together. As with many Atlantic stereo releases of this era, Tom Dowd hard-panned much of the instrumentation – a property of the original mix, not a reissue's fault.

**Best early pressing for audiophiles:** [Atlantic SD 1305](https://www.discogs.com/release/5923976) – 1960 original pressing, green/blue "bullseye" label with fan logo.

**Best modern pressing for audiophiles:** [Analogue Productions APA 001-45](https://www.discogs.com/release/28538188) (2023) – mastered by Kevin Gray at Cohearent Audio from the original tape, part of the Atlantic 75 series.

**Best-value modern:** [London/Atlantic UK pressing](https://www.discogs.com/release/5113505) (Decca manufactured, 1960) – a reasonable alternate when a US original is scarce or overpriced.

## Buyer's guide by budget

**$$ (Affordable):** The [**London/Atlantic UK pressing**](https://www.discogs.com/release/5113505), or the [**Rhino reissue**](https://www.discogs.com/release/2006252) if you specifically want the bonus alternate takes.

**$$$ (Mid-range):** A clean [**Atlantic SD 1305**](https://www.discogs.com/release/5923976) original, condition-dependent.

**$$$$$ (Premium):** The [**Analogue Productions APA 001-45**](https://www.discogs.com/release/28538188) 45rpm reissue – Michael Fremer's TrackingAngle review rated it 9/11 for both music and sound.
