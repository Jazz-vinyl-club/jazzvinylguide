# Conference of the Birds on Vinyl: The Definitive Pressing Guide

## Summary

*Conference of the Birds* was recorded on November 30, 1972 at Allegro Studio in New York, engineered by Tony May and produced by Manfred Eicher. It was Dave Holland's fourth project for ECM and his first as sole leader. The quartet is Holland on bass, Sam Rivers and Anthony Braxton on reeds and flutes, and Barry Altschul on percussion. ECM released it in 1973 as ECM 1027 ST.

The whole album runs under 40 minutes and was finished in one six-hour session. The buying decision is unusual for a reissue-heavy catalogue. Owner reports say the 2017 ECM reissue was pressed from the 1973 stampers rather than recut. That puts it closer to a late original pressing than to a remaster.

**Best early pressing for audiophiles:** A [1973 ECM 1027](https://www.discogs.com/release/12341594) original, yellow cover with green labels.

**Best modern pressing for audiophiles:** The 2017 ECM 180g reissue, reported by owners to use the original 1973 stampers.

**Best-value modern:** A [1976 or 1977 German reissue](https://www.discogs.com/release/7661254), identified by "Urheber- Und..." in the label rim text.

## Recording history and tape provenance

This was the only time Rivers and Braxton recorded together. Holland and Altschul had played together since 1970 in a trio with Chick Corea, and Braxton joined them that August in the group Circle.

All six compositions are Holland's. Each is open form. A theme at the start sets key, tempo and mood, then the players improvise freely until the theme returns. Stuart Nicholson describes the result as a return to the rugged discipline of early 1960s free improvising.

Holland's liner note explains the title. He had a small garden at his London apartment. Birds gathered there one by one at dawn in summer, each declaring its freedom in song. He wanted to share that spirit with the other musicians.

## Original ECM pressings (1973)

The [1973 original](https://www.discogs.com/release/12341594) has the yellow cover and green labels, with ECM 1027 ST on the sleeve and ECM 1027 on the labels. Its back cover reads "Printed in W. Germany" and the labels read "Made in Germany." A near-identical variant omits the "Made in Germany" line.

Some copies of the original have die-cuts in the back cover, so the sleeve can stand up like a photo frame. That affects how cover condition should be judged.

The [US pressing](https://www.discogs.com/release/1741961-David-Holland-Quartet-Conference-Of-The-Birds) was distributed by Polydor in New York and printed in the USA. Its runouts are stamped, with etched plate identifiers. No published comparison with the German pressing has been found.

A [German reissue from late 1976 or early 1977](https://www.discogs.com/release/7661254) carries "Urheber- Und..." rim text and a label code. That is how it is told apart from the first pressings. It is a vintage-era alternative at a lower price.

## The 2017 ECM reissue

Several owner reviews on the [Discogs master page](https://www.discogs.com/master/71747-David-Holland-Quartet-David-Holland-Sam-Rivers-Anthony-Braxton-Barry-Altschul-Conference-Of-The-Bird) state that the 2017 180g reissue was pressed from the same stampers cut in 1973. On that account it carries the original mastering with no new lacquer cut and no digital step.

The same reviewers on that [master page](https://www.discogs.com/master/71747-David-Holland-Quartet-David-Holland-Sam-Rivers-Anthony-Braxton-Barry-Altschul-Conference-Of-The-Bird) describe the pressing as dead quiet, which matters on a record with very quiet passages. One rates it ahead of comparable Acoustic Sounds and Tone Poet reissues from other labels.

ECM has not published a statement confirming the stamper claim, so it rests on owner reports rather than the label's own description.

## Buyer's guide by budget

**$$:** A [1976 or 1977 German reissue](https://www.discogs.com/release/7661254), identified by the rim text.

**$$$:** The 2017 ECM 180g reissue, for quiet surfaces and, per owner reports, the original mastering.

**$$$$:** A clean [1973 ECM 1027](https://www.discogs.com/release/12341594) original, priced on condition.
