# The Real McCoy on Vinyl: The Definitive Pressing Guide

## Summary

*The Real McCoy* was recorded on April 21, 1967 at Van Gelder Studio in Englewood Cliffs, New Jersey. Alfred Lion produced and Rudy Van Gelder engineered. It was McCoy Tyner's first album as a Blue Note leader, two years after leaving the John Coltrane Quartet. Tyner plays piano with Joe Henderson on tenor saxophone, Ron Carter on bass and Elvin Jones on drums. Blue Note released it in 1967 as BLP 4264 in mono and BST 84264 in stereo.

One thing about this title trips up buyers more than the usual original-against-reissue question. The genuine first pressing already carries Liberty's blue and white labels rather than the "New York, USA" Blue Note design collectors associate with originals. Sellers sometimes price those copies as a lesser later pressing when they are the first edition.

**Best early pressing for audiophiles:** The [Liberty-label first pressing](https://www.discogs.com/release/424427-McCoy-Tyner-The-Real-McCoy), BST 84264, with the Van Gelder stamp.

**Best modern pressing for audiophiles:** The [Music Matters 45rpm 2LP](https://www.discogs.com/release/3378635-McCoy-Tyner-The-Real-McCoy) (2011), mastered by Kevin Gray and Steve Hoffman at AcousTech.

**Best-value modern:** The [Blue Note Classic Vinyl Edition](https://www.discogs.com/release/16316769-McCoy-Tyner-The-Real-McCoy), mastered by Kevin Gray, inspected on arrival.

## Why the Liberty label is the original here

Liberty Records acquired Blue Note in 1965 and 1966. By the time this session was recorded and released in 1967, the Liberty label design was already standard across the catalogue.

So the blue and white labels reading "A Division of Liberty Records, Inc." are correct for a 1967 first pressing of this title. The runouts read BNST-84264-A VAN GELDER and BNST-84264-B VAN GELDER, confirming Van Gelder's original lacquer cut.

A pressing-plant variant has "114" etched in the runout, indicating an All Disc pressing from the same metalwork rather than a later cut.

This is first-generation metalwork with a Van Gelder cut, carrying the label design Blue Note used company-wide by 1967. Near-mint copies are collectible, and a discount on the label design alone is a mispricing rather than a bargain.

## Recording history and tape provenance

Tyner spent five years as the pianist in John Coltrane's classic quartet, helping define the modal vocabulary that shaped 1960s jazz. He left in late 1965, uncomfortable with the direction Coltrane's music was taking.

The next two years were professionally uncertain. Tyner has spoken about considering leaving music altogether and driving a cab. This session marked his return to visibility. Lion recalled it as a pure jazz session with a deep love for the music in each selection.

All five tracks are Tyner originals. "Passion Dance" opens at full intensity, with Tyner's rhythmic upheaval and Jones's polyrhythms making an obstacle course for Henderson's tenor.

"Contemplation" and "Search for Peace" are the two ballads, which Tyner has described in spiritual terms. He framed the second around a man's submission to God and the giving over of the self.

"Four by Five" is a knotty excursion in 5/4. The closing "Blues on the Corner" is a loose blues tribute to Tyner's Philadelphia childhood.

Carter came on loan from the Miles Davis Quintet, and Jones was reunited with his former Coltrane bandmate.

## Music Matters 45rpm (2011)

The [Music Matters edition](https://www.discogs.com/release/3378635-McCoy-Tyner-The-Real-McCoy), catalogue MMBST-84264, is a 2×180g set at 45rpm in a gatefold. Kevin Gray and Steve Hoffman remastered it at AcousTech from the original Van Gelder stereo session tapes, and it was pressed at RTI.

This release predates Music Matters' move to Gray's own Cohearent Audio facility, so both engineers are credited. Owners single out Elvin Jones's drums and cymbals for separation. It is out of print.

## Blue Note Classic Vinyl Edition

The [Classic Vinyl Edition](https://www.discogs.com/release/16316769-McCoy-Tyner-The-Real-McCoy) is a 180g LP mastered by Kevin Gray at Cohearent Audio from the original master tapes.

[Blue Note president Don Was](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hWx30qQXc_c) named this title as one of the two launch albums for the Classic Vinyl series, alongside Lee Morgan's *The Sidewinder*. The series extends the label's 80th-anniversary reissue programme.

Owner reports split about evenly. Several describe flat, silent, punchy copies at a bargain price. A comparable number report dished vinyl, visible dirt on arrival and audible pops, particularly on side two.

One owner notes the construction is budget by design, with a simple paper inner sleeve and no tip-on jacket, even where the mastering draws praise. A seller with an easy return policy matters here.

This title has not been issued in Blue Note's Tone Poet series, unlike Tyner's *Time For Tyner*, *Expansions* and *Extensions*.

## Japanese and later pressings

[King Records GXF-3008](https://www.discogs.com/release/3937192-McCoy-Tyner-The-Real-McCoy) (1977) is a licensed Japanese pressing from King's Blue Note series, with a period-correct obi. As with other King-era licences, owners report quiet vinyl.

[Later pressings from the 1970s to 1987](https://www.discogs.com/release/7314300-McCoy-Tyner-The-Real-McCoy) vary in quality, including a 1987 DMM cut from Europadisk.

## Pressings to avoid

No bootleg or notably compromised pressing of this title is widely documented.

The Classic Vinyl quality-control variance described here is a manufacturing consistency problem rather than a distinct bad pressing to identify and avoid.

## Buyer's guide by budget

**$:** The [Blue Note Classic Vinyl Edition](https://www.discogs.com/release/16316769-McCoy-Tyner-The-Real-McCoy), bought with a return policy.

**$$:** A [Japanese King pressing](https://www.discogs.com/release/3937192-McCoy-Tyner-The-Real-McCoy) with obi.

**$$$$:** The [Music Matters 45rpm 2LP](https://www.discogs.com/release/3378635-McCoy-Tyner-The-Real-McCoy) on the secondary market, or a [Liberty-label first pressing](https://www.discogs.com/release/424427-McCoy-Tyner-The-Real-McCoy) in honest condition.
